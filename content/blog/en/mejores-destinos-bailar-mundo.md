If you love dancing salsa and bachata, you've probably dreamed of travelling to those legendary places where Latin music plays on every corner.

As a teacher who has travelled and danced across half the world, I'm going to tell you **exactly where to go, when to go, and what to expect**.

## The Must-Visit Destinations

### 1. 🇨🇺 Havana, Cuba — The Birthplace of Salsa

**Why go**:

Cuban salsa is the most authentic in the world

Live music everywhere

Classes with legendary masters

Very affordable prices

**Best time**: November – April (avoid hurricane season)

**Recommended level**: Intermediate in salsa

**Daily budget**: 30–50€

**What to dance**:

🎶 Cuban salsa (timba)

🎶 Cuban son

🎶 Rumba

Bachata (less common, but it's around)

**Best places**:

**Casa de la Música Habana**: The temple of salsa

**Jardines de 1830**: Sea view, live music

**Callejón de Hamel**: Sunday rumba (NOT TO BE MISSED!)

**Café Cantante**: Inside the National Theatre

**Insider tips**:

➖ Cubans dance FAST — be prepared

🎶 Bring cash; cards don't work well

🎶 Learn some "salsa casino" before you go

🎶 Hire local dancers as guides (30–40€/day)

**Best part**: Absolute authenticity

**Downside**: Limited tourist infrastructure

### 2. 🇨🇴 Cali, Colombia — The World Capital of Salsa

**Why go**:

"The capital of salsa" for good reason

Caleños live and breathe salsa

World-class academies

Caleña salsa (unique style)

**Best time**: Year-round (stable climate)

**Recommended level**: Intermediate–Advanced

**Daily budget**: 40–70€

**What to dance**:

🎶 Caleña salsa (on1 with incredible footwork)

🎶 Linear salsa

Bachata (increasingly popular)

**Best places**:

**Tin Tin Deo**: Legendary — classes + social

**Zaperoco**: Authentic salsa bar

**La Topa Tolondra**: Traditional

**Mulato Cabaret**: Spectacular shows

**Academies**: Swing Latino, Son de Luz

**Insider tips**:

🎶 Caleños are the BEST dancers in the world

🎶 The footwork is breathtaking

🎶 Take classes specifically in "salsa caleña"

🎶 Visit the Salsa Monument (Sebastián de Belalcázar)

**Best part**: Extremely high technical level

**Downside**: Can be intimidating for beginners

### 3. 🇩🇴 Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — The Home of Bachata

**Why go**:

THE birthplace of bachata

Authentic Dominican bachata

Live bachata musicians

Fusion with merengue

**Best time**: December – April

**Recommended level**: All levels

**Daily budget**: 50–80€

**What to dance**:

🎶 Dominican bachata (traditional)

🎶 Modern bachata

🎶 Merengue

Salsa (also popular)

**Best places**:

**Jet Set**: The most famous club

**Maunaloa**: Colonial Zone

**Onno's Bar**: Bachata and jazz

**Coliseo de Bachata**: Cultural Centre

**Insider tips**:

🎶 Dominican bachata is different from European sensual bachata

🎶 Learn the "típico" (Dominican footwork)

🎶 Dominicans dance VERY fast

🎶 Visit during the "Bachata Festival" (October)

**Best part**: The most authentic bachata possible

**Downside**: European "sensual bachata" is less common there

### 4. 🗽 New York, USA — The Capital of Salsa On2

**Why go**:

Salsa on2 (mambo style) was born here

Incredible multicultural scene

International congresses

Very high technical level

**Best time**: April – October (avoid the brutal winter)

**Recommended level**: Intermediate–Advanced

**Daily budget**: 100–200€ (expensive)

**What to dance**:

🎶 Salsa on2 (Mambo)

🎶 Salsa on1

🎶 Bachata (very popular)

🎶 Kizomba

**Best places**:

**SOBs (Sounds of Brazil)**: Legendary

**Taj Lounge**: Salsa Thursdays

**Gonzalez y Gonzalez**: Mondays "Salsa, Bachata & Tequila"

**Latin Quarters**: Multiple nights

**Insider tips**:

🎶 "Salsa on2" is the dominant style

🎶 VERY high level — practise before you go

🎶 Expensive entry (15–30$ per event)

🎶 New Yorkers take their dancing seriously

**Best part**: Diversity and technical level

**Downside**: Very expensive, can be intimidating

### 5. 🇵🇷 San Juan, Puerto Rico — The Island of Enchantment

**Why go**:

Perfect mix of salsa and reggaeton

Relaxed Caribbean atmosphere

Constant live music

Beaches + dancing = perfection

**Best time**: December – April

**Recommended level**: All levels

**Daily budget**: 70–120€

**What to dance**:

🎶 Puerto Rican salsa

🎶 Bachata

🎶 Reggaeton

🎶 Merengue

**Best places**:

**La Placita de Santurce**: Thursday–Sunday

**Nuyorican Café**: Live music

**Barrachina**: Historic (they invented the piña colada)

**La Factoría**: Multi-level bar

**Insider tips**:

🎶 La Placita is EPIC on Thursday nights

🎶 Puerto Ricans mix salsa with reggaeton

🎶 Very tourist-friendly

🎶 They speak both Spanish and English

**Best part**: Relaxed, festive atmosphere

**Downside**: Can be expensive

### 6. 🇪🇸 Barcelona, Spain — Europe's Bachata Capital

**Why go**:

The largest bachata scene in Europe

Constant international congresses

Mix of styles (sensual, Dominican, modern)

Perfect Mediterranean climate

**Best time**: Year-round (ideal April–October)

**Recommended level**: All levels

**Daily budget**: 60–100€

**What to dance**:

🎶 Sensual bachata (dominant)

🎶 Dominican bachata

🎶 Salsa

🎶 Kizomba

**Best places**:

**Mojito Barcelona**: Several venues

**Marula Café**: Raval, Afro-Caribbean music

**Danzatoria**: Weekly events

**Antilla Barcelona**: Large Latin club

**Insider tips**:

🎶 Barcelona has a bachata event EVERY night

🎶 European "sensual" bachata is different from Dominican

🎶 Many international artists come to teach

🎶 Join WhatsApp bachata groups

**Best part**: Excellent tourist infrastructure

**Downside**: Can feel overly touristy at times

### 7. 🇫🇷 Paris, France — A Latin Surprise

**Why go**:

Second-largest Latin scene in Europe

Unique France-Latino fusion

World-class classes

Very social community

**Best time**: April – October

**Recommended level**: Intermediate

**Daily budget**: 80–150€

**What to dance**:

🎶 Sensual bachata (very popular)

🎶 Salsa

🎶 Kizomba (very big here)

**Best places**:

**Noche Latina at Carpe Diem**: Fridays

**La Casbah**: Bachata on Thursdays

**Favela Chic**: Latin-Brazilian fusion

**Insider tips**:

🎶 The French are VERY good at kizomba

🎶 Events tend to start late (11pm+)

🎶 Many people in the dance scene speak English

**Best part**: Surprisingly high level and great atmosphere

**Downside**: Paris is expensive

### 8. 🇲🇽 Mexico City, Mexico — Latin Giant

**Why go**:

Huge and diverse scene

Super affordable prices

Fusion with cumbia and norteña

Mexicans are incredibly friendly

**Best time**: October – May

**Recommended level**: All levels

**Daily budget**: 30–60€

**What to dance**:

🎶 Salsa

🎶 Bachata

🎶 Cumbia

🎶 Norteña

**Best places**:

**Mama Rumba**: Legendary

**Pepe Toño**: Zona Roma

**New Garibaldi**: Mariachis + salsa

**Patrick Miller**: Retro salsa

**Insider tips**:

🎶 The food is incredible and cheap

🎶 Mexicans mix many styles

🎶 Very safe in tourist areas

🎶 Altitude (2,240m) — hydrate extra

**Best part**: Price, food, friendly people

**Downside**: Pollution in the city

## Tips for Dancing Abroad

### Before the Trip

🎶 Join Facebook groups for the city

🎶 Download apps: **Bachataworld**, **SalsaDancingLA**

🎶 Learn a few basic phrases in the local language

🎶 Check the events calendar

🎶 Book workshops/classes in advance

### During the Trip

🎶 Arrive early at venues (you'll meet people)

🎶 Ask locals to dance (learn their regional style)

🎶 Be humble (every country has its own style)

🎶 Exchange Instagram with new friends

🎶 Record videos (not excessively, but some are fine)

### Precautions

➖ Check the safety of the neighbourhood

➖ Keep your drink in sight

➖ Uber/taxi back to the hotel

➖ Copy of your passport on your phone

➖ Hotel card in your pocket
