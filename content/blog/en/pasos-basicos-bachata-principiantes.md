Want to learn to dance bachata but don't know where to start? Don't worry — we've all been there. In this tutorial, I'll teach you the fundamental steps you need to start dancing with confidence.

## What Is Bachata?

Bachata is a **Dominican social dance** characterised by its romantic rhythm and sensual hip movements. It's danced with a partner and is one of the most popular Latin dances in the world.

### Key characteristics:

- **Rhythm**: 4 beats with a "tap" on beat 4

- **Movement**: Lateral or in a box pattern

- **Style**: Romantic and sensual

- **Music**: Guitars, bongos, and love songs

## Before You Start: Preparation

### Correct Posture

1. **Back straight** but relaxed

2. **Knees slightly bent**

3. **Weight on the balls of your feet**

4. **Shoulders relaxed and back**

5. **Eyes forward** (not on the floor)

### Pro Tip:

> Posture is 50% of the dance. Good posture makes any step look better.

## Step 1: The Side Basic (Individual)

This is the most fundamental step. Practise it until it becomes automatic.

### For the Leader (usually the man):

**Beat 1**: Side step left with the left foot
**Beat 2**: Right foot closes to the left
**Beat 3**: Side step left with the left foot
**Beat 4**: Tap with the right foot (touches the floor without transferring weight) + hip movement

**Beat 5**: Side step right with the right foot
**Beat 6**: Left foot closes to the right
**Beat 7**: Side step right with the right foot
**Beat 8**: Tap with the left foot + hip movement

### For the Follower (usually the woman):

It's exactly the same but **you start with the right foot** (mirror of the leader).

**Beat 1**: Side step right with the right foot
**Beat 2**: Left foot closes to the right
**Beat 3**: Side step right with the right foot
**Beat 4**: Tap with the left foot + hip

**Beat 5**: Side step left with the left foot
**Beat 6**: Right foot closes to the left
**Beat 7**: Side step left with the left foot
**Beat 8**: Tap with the right foot + hip

### Practice:

Spend **at least 10 minutes** on just this step with slow music. Count out loud: "1, 2, 3, tap — 5, 6, 7, tap"

## Step 2: The Hip Movement

The hip movement is what gives bachata its "flavour".

### Technique:

1. On beat 4 (the tap), **slightly bend the knee** of your supporting leg

2. This will cause your hip to move **naturally** towards that side

3. **Don't force** the movement — it should be smooth and natural

4. The hip "drops" towards the side of the supporting leg

### Common Mistake:

➖ Moving the hip using the upper body

🎶 The movement comes from the knees, not the torso

## Step 3: The Partner Basic (Closed Position)

Now let's apply the basic with your partner.

### Grip Position:

**Leader:**

- Right hand on the follower's back (shoulder blade)

- Left hand holds the follower's right hand

- Elbows at 90 degrees

- Arms firm but not rigid

**Follower:**

- Left hand on the leader's shoulder/bicep

- Right hand in the leader's left hand

- Keep your own weight (don't lean on your partner)

### Executing the Basic:

1. Leader starts with the left foot, follower with the right

2. They move as a **mirror** (when the leader goes left, the follower goes right)

3. Maintain **occasional eye contact** (don't look at your feet)

4. Breathe and relax

## Step 4: Your First Turn (Right Turn for the Follower)

Once you've mastered the basic, it's time for your first turn.

### From the Closed Position:

**Beats 1–3**: Normal basic
**Beat 4**: Leader raises their left arm (turn signal)

**Beats 5–7**: Follower turns 360° to their right while continuing the basic
**Beat 8**: Turn ends facing the leader

### Tips for the Turn:

- **Follower**: Keep your basic while turning (don't stop your feet)

- **Leader**: Raise the arm high enough but don't force it

- **Both**: The turn should be smooth, not rushed

- **Spotting**: Fix your gaze on one point to avoid dizziness

## Common Mistakes and How to Fix Them

### Mistake 1: Looking at Your Feet

**Solution**: Practise in front of a mirror. Count the beats out loud.

### Mistake 2: Being Tense

**Solution**: Breathe deeply. Remember this is meant to be fun, not a test.

### Mistake 3: Not Listening to the Music

**Solution**: Listen to bachata in your own time. Identify the beats.

### Mistake 4: Going Too Fast

**Solution**: Practise with slow music first. Speed comes on its own.

## Recommended Songs to Practise With

These songs have a **clear rhythm and slow tempo**, perfect for beginners:

1. **"Obsesión"** — Aventura (classic and slow)

2. **"Dile al Amor"** — Aventura (very clear rhythm)

3. **"Propuesta Indecente"** — Romeo Santos (medium tempo)

4. **"Bella y Sensual"** — Romeo Santos & Daddy Yankee (faster)

## Weekly Practice Routine

To progress quickly, I recommend:

**Days 1–2**: 20 min individual side basic

**Days 3–4**: 20 min partner basic

**Days 5–6**: 15 min turns + 10 min freestyle

**Day 7**: Go out and dance socially!

## Next Level: What to Learn After This

Once you've mastered these fundamentals:

- Left turn

- Full turn (enchufla)

- Hand changes

- Mirror steps

- Torso movements (body rolls)
