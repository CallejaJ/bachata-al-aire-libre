Málaga has become one of the most important destinations for bachata and salsa lovers in Spain. With its perfect climate, welcoming atmosphere, and growing community of dancers, the city has options for every level and taste.

As a bachata teacher who has been part of the Málaga scene for years, I'm going to share the best places to dance, learn, and enjoy Latin music in our beautiful city.

## 🌟 Classes and Schools

### 1. Bachata al Aire Libre (My Classes!)

**Location**: Various parks in Málaga
**Style**: Sensual Bachata and Dominican Bachata
**Level**: All levels
**Schedule**: Afternoons/Evenings (check availability)

**Why is it special?**

- Outdoor classes (first day free!)

- Relaxed, pressure-free atmosphere

- Small groups

- Occasional live music

- Social practice after class

**Price**: Very affordable, with multi-class passes available

**Contact**: +34 698 50 16 76 / bachataalairelibrelibre@gmail.com

### 2. Dance Emotion Málaga

**Location**: Málaga city centre
**Styles**: Bachata, Salsa, Kizomba
**Level**: Beginner to Advanced

One of the most established schools in Málaga. They offer structured classes and train competition teams.

**Advantages:**

- Experienced teachers

- Spacious facilities

- Flexible timetables

- Active community

### 3. Bailonga Málaga

**Location**: Pedregalejo
**Speciality**: Cuban Salsa and On2
**Atmosphere**: Very social

If you like salsa, this is your place. They have a very tight-knit community and organise regular social outings.

## 🎉 Weekly Events and Socials

### Monday: Social at El Pimpi

**Time**: 21:00 – 00:00
**Entry**: Free
**Music**: Salsa, Bachata, Kizomba

Touristy atmosphere but with a good level of dancing. Perfect for meeting people from all over the world.

### Tuesday: Bachata Night at La Tranca

**Time**: 22:00 – 03:00
**Entry**: 5€ (includes one drink)
**Music**: 80% Bachata, 20% Salsa

The Tuesday night for Málaga's bachata community. Resident DJ with an excellent music selection.

### Wednesday: Salsa & Bachata at Anden 36

**Time**: 21:30 – 02:00
**Entry**: 3€ before 23:00, 5€ after
**Music**: Balanced mix

Large venue with two floors. Beginners welcome.

### Thursday: Tropical Thursdays at Sala Gold

**Time**: 23:00 – 04:00
**Entry**: 8€
**Music**: Salsa, Bachata, Reggaeton

Younger, more energetic crowd. Good music but can get very busy.

### Friday: Latino Fridays at El Rincón del Arte

**Time**: 23:00 – 05:00
**Entry**: 10€
**Music**: All kinds of Latin

The biggest event of the week. Dancers from all over Andalusia. Two floors: one for salsa/bachata and one for urban.

### Saturday: Latin Saturday at ZZ Pub

**Time**: 23:30 – 06:00
**Entry**: 12€
**Music**: Varied

Large club with great sound system. Gets very full but worth it.

### Sunday: Sunday Social at Pepe Mulero

**Time**: 20:00 – 00:00
**Entry**: Free
**Music**: Classic Salsa and Bachata

More relaxed and family-friendly atmosphere. Perfect for practising without pressure.

## 🏖️ Outdoor Events (Summer)

### Málaga Beach Salsa Festival

**When**: July
**Where**: La Malagueta beach
**Entry**: Free

Classes, shows and social dancing on the beach. Not to be missed!

### Bachata Nights in the Park

**When**: June – September (Fridays)
**Where**: Various parks
**Entry**: Free

Organised by different schools. Check the calendar on social media.

### Málaga International Festival

**When**: September
**Where**: Conference Centre
**Entry**: Registration required

Workshops, competitions and parties over an entire weekend.

## 🎵 Bars with Live Latin Music

### 1. El Mojito Bar (Marbella, nearby)

**Music**: Live Thursday–Saturday
**Style**: Salsa, Cuban Son
**Atmosphere**: Authentic Caribbean

Worth the trip to Marbella. Incredible Cuban musicians.

### 2. La Botega de la Abuela

**Music**: Live on Saturdays
**Style**: Flamenco-Latin fusion
**Atmosphere**: Intimate

Small but full of energy. Book a table.

### 3. Bodega El Pimpi

**Music**: Occasional
**Style**: Varied
**Atmosphere**: Touristy but charming

More for drinks than dancing, but there are sometimes surprises.

## 💡 Tips for Enjoying the Málaga Scene

### For Beginners:

1. **Start with classes**: Don't go straight to socials
2. **Arrive early**: Events usually include a class beforehand
3. **Don't be afraid**: Everyone was a beginner once
4. **Ask people to dance**: The community is very welcoming

### For Visitors:

1. **Check the calendar**: Events change depending on the season
2. **Join WhatsApp groups**: There are several for the community
3. **Stay in the centre**: You'll be able to walk to many places
4. **Learn some Spanish**: Though many people speak English

### For Everyone:

1. **Stay hydrated**: Málaga can get very hot
2. **Dress comfortably**: Appropriate shoes for dancing
3. **Respect social etiquette**: Thank each partner after a dance
4. **Network**: The best experiences come from friendships

- Highest concentration of bars
- Easy to get around on foot
- Tourist atmosphere

### Pedregalejo

- More local and authentic
- Close to the beach
- Easier parking

### University Area

- Younger crowd
- Lower prices
- More informal events

## 🚗 Getting Around

**Best option**: Uber/Cabify

- Taxis are hard to find at weekends
- Parking in the centre is tricky
- Night bus service is limited

**Plan B**: Bicycle

- Málaga has a good cycle lane network
- Many venues have bike parking
