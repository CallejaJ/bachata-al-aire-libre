Málaga is one of the most active cruise ports in the Mediterranean. Every day, hundreds of passengers arrive with just a few hours to discover the city and take home a memory that isn't just photos of the Alcazaba.

If you're the organiser of your group, or simply looking for something different from the usual tours, this is for you.

## Why a Bachata Class Is the Perfect Excursion

Most cruise excursions are the same: coach, guide, monument, photo, souvenir shop.

An outdoor bachata class in the centre of Málaga is something else entirely:

**Active** — you're moving, laughing and working up a sweat (just a little — it's a class, not a marathon)

**Social** — ideal for groups who already know each other, or for breaking the ice between strangers

**Authentic** — bachata is the popular music of the Mediterranean and Latin America, and Málaga has a very vibrant scene

**Photogenic** — photos of you dancing outdoors in Málaga are unbeatable

**No requirements** — no prior experience needed, no partner, no special outfit

## How Does a Cruise Group Class Work?

### Duration and Format

I work with groups in sessions of **60 to 90 minutes**, designed specifically for people who have never danced or have danced very little.

The typical structure:

**First 10 minutes**: Warm-up and first basic steps, individually. Everyone at the same pace, no pressure.

**Next 30–40 minutes**: We learn the bachata foundation — the side step, the hip movement, and a couple of simple figures. Always with a partner, rotating so that everyone dances with everyone.

**Last 15–20 minutes**: A full song danced by the group, with live music or a DJ. This is when the best photos happen.

### Group Size

I comfortably work with groups of **8 to 40 people**.

For larger groups, I can coordinate with other instructors from the local scene to ensure the experience remains just as personal.

### Location

Classes are held outdoors in iconic areas of central Málaga:

Parque de Málaga (10 minutes' walk from the port)

The La Malagueta area

Squares in the historic city centre

All locations are easily reachable from the port on foot or by taxi in under 15 minutes.

## Is Prior Experience Required?

**No.** And I mean that without any qualifications.

I have taught cruise group classes to participants aged between 18 and 75, from more than 20 different nationalities, with zero dancing knowledge. By the end of the class, everyone dances a full song.

The secret is that bachata has one of the fastest learning curves of all partner dances. With 4 beats, a side step, and basic connection, you can already impress.

### What if some people in the group are older or have limited mobility?

No problem. The intensity is completely adapted to the group. There are no jumps, no forced positions. The movement is gentle, controlled, and at your own pace.

## What You Need to Bring

Comfortable clothing (whatever you're wearing is fine)

Flat-soled footwear if possible (no thin-strap sandals or stilettos)

A positive attitude and willingness to have fun

That's it.

## How to Organise It: Logistics for Cruise Groups

I know perfectly well that cruise groups have very tight schedules. That's why I adapt to your timetable, not the other way around.

### What I need from you:

**Approximate number of people** — so I can prepare the space and arrange additional support if needed

**Port arrival time and latest time back on board** — to work out exactly how much time we have

**Group language** — I teach in Spanish, English, and some French and German. For groups with other languages, I work with an interpreter or with visual resources

**Organiser's contact details** — to confirm everything the day before

### Book in Advance

Málaga is a very busy port, especially in high season (April–October). To guarantee availability and the outdoor space, ideally book at least **48–72 hours in advance**.

For large groups or special dates (Easter Week, summer, long weekends), the sooner the better.

## Frequently Asked Questions from Cruise Groups

### What if it rains?

Málaga has more than 300 sunny days a year. If it exceptionally rains, I have covered indoor alternatives in the city centre.

### Can we film video during the class?

Absolutely. In fact, I encourage someone in the group to be the "official documentarian". Videos of groups dancing bachata for the first time always go down a storm on social media.

### Is there live music?

Live percussion can be arranged for groups who want a more premium experience. Just ask me when booking.

### Does the price include transfers?

No, but the meeting point is chosen to be convenient from the port. I'll give you exact directions when confirming the booking.

### Can it be combined with another activity?

Yes — groups often combine the class with a food tour, a visit to the Picasso Museum, or free time in the city centre. The bachata class works well both at the start and at the end of the day.
